📊 WHAT WAS FIXED

ORIGINAL PROBLEM:
  ❌ Win Rate: 29.9% (too low)
  ❌ Trade Count: 230 (too many = 6.2/day)
  ❌ Entry Logic: Only checked ML signal
  ✅ Risk Management: 1.09% drawdown (excellent!)

ROOT CAUSE:
  ML threshold too loose (0.5%) + no technical confirmation
  → Entering too many marginal trades
  → Low win rate despite good risk management


🎯 THE FIX
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

CHANGE 1: Confluence Filter (NEW)
  Added multi-signal validation checking 5 indicators:
  ✓ RSI (momentum)
  ✓ MACD (trend)
  ✓ Volume (participation)
  ✓ Bollinger Bands (volatility)
  ✓ EMA Trend (direction)
  
  Result: Confluence Score (0 to 1)
  - Filters out trades when only 1 signal aligns
  - Accepts trades when 2+ signals align

CHANGE 2: ML Threshold Increase
  From: 0.5% (0.005)
  To:   0.8% (0.008)
  Effect: Filters weak predictions

CHANGE 3: Dual-Condition Entry
  BEFORE: if ml_signal > 0.5%: enter()
  AFTER:  if ml_signal > 0.8% AND confluence > 0.4: enter()

CHANGE 4: Risk Management
  ✅ UNCHANGED - Kept 100% of proven risk control
  ✅ Kelly Criterion position sizing
  ✅ Custom stoploss with profit protection
  ✅ Portfolio heat limits (6%)
  ✅ Drawdown-based position scaling
